Two classifiers, one integration platform

Ada decides what your history has already decided. Jev answers from pretraining. Boomi moves the case between them.

Glyphh
Ada a classifier that learns from precedent
A new case is answered from the nearest decisions you already made, the way case law works.
  • 01Learns from your record. Record a case and its outcome; it counts the moment it is written. No dataset, no training run, no retrain.
  • 02Typed answer, calibrated confidence. One of the outcomes your spec allows, a probability for each, and the precedents it stands on.
  • 03No language model in the path. Hyperdimensional computing: the same case gets the same answer, in milliseconds.
  • 04Knows when to ask. Below your bar the case goes to a person or an LLM, and their answer becomes the next precedent.
TypeSafe AI
Jev a typed-output model
Ask any number of typed questions about one state in one request; it answers from what it learned in pretraining.
  • 01Typed outputs, not prose. A choice, a number, a yes or no, with a probability, so software can act on it directly.
  • 02Many questions per call. One state in, fourteen answers out, as in TypeSafe's security workflow.
  • 03Keeps nothing between calls. Your examples travel inside each request and are paid for every time, up to its context limit.
  • 04Fast and cheap per call. Around a fifth of a second and fractions of a cent, starting from general knowledge rather than your record.

Ada as a decision step in a process

One HTTP Client connector to Ada Cloud. No model, no retrain, no new system of record.

01

A case arrives

A ticket, a claim, an order exception or an alert lands through any Boomi connector: Salesforce, ServiceNow, NetSuite, SAP, email.

02

Map the fields

A Boomi map turns the record into the fields Ada's question asks about: department, amount, category, the text itself.

03

Ada decides

The HTTP Client connector calls Ada Cloud. Back come the outcome, a calibrated confidence and the precedents behind it, in about 100 ms.

Ada Cloud
04

Decision shape

Above your bar, the process acts: assign the team, approve the claim, update the system of record. Below it, a Boomi Flow task for a person, or an LLM step.

05

Record the outcome

Whoever decided, the final outcome is recorded back to Ada. The next similar case is answered from it, with no retrain.

→ Ada
