# Glyphh Desktop — hero demo script (scripted screen capture)

One continuous take in the real app, ~45–60s, looping cleanly back to its
first frame. Same five beats as the first-run tour and the product page:
Connections → Apps → Loops → Layouts → Command center. No narration — the
motion tells the story; the page copy around the video does the talking.
Record at the page's hero resolution, cursor visible, unhurried pace
(every beat readable at a glance before the next begins).

## Beat 1 — Connections (~8s)
Cold open on the Connections page: a healthy grid of connected accounts
(Gmail, Slack, Drive, Calendar visible). Click ONE connect action so the
connect flow flashes on screen (cut before any real credential UI). The
point: wired once, ready everywhere.

## Beat 2 — Apps (~8s)
⌘⇧Space → click Gmail in the dock. The inbox opens as a panel. `.dip slack`
in the composer → Slack replaces it (the pinned-panel swap on screen). The
point: your apps live beside the agent, one keystroke or one command away.

## Beat 3 — Loops (~10s)
Open the agent (Glyphh mark). Click the loop chip → pick "Plan & Build";
tap the model chip so the Planner/Builder pairing shows (Fable 5 / an
open-weight worker). Type a real ask — "clear the inbox and draft replies
to the three urgent ones" — hit Enter, let two tool steps stream in.
The point: a frontier model directs, workers execute, visibly.

## Beat 4 — Layouts (~10s)
⌘⇧Space → click a saved "Morning" layout: the workspace REORIENTS in one
motion — inbox + calendar + agent, folder and session snapping with it.
Then click a "Build" layout: agent + terminal + browser. The point: one
click per job, the whole workspace follows.

## Beat 5 — Command center (~8s)
⌘⇧Space one last time, held on screen a moment: the dock (Glyphh first),
the grid, a press-and-hold jiggle + one tile drag to the dock. Click
Glyphh → land back on the agent, composer ready — END FRAME matches the
opening tone so the loop reads clean.

## Capture notes
- Fresh demo org: real-looking data, nothing sensitive (no live tokens,
  no real inbox contents — staged accounts only).
- Hide the OS menu bar/dock; app full-screen; default dark theme.
- Each beat begins with ~0.5s of stillness — the page's section anchors
  may deep-link to timestamps later.
- Deliver as glyphh-desktop.mp4, same resolution/loop treatment as the
  current hero (drop-in replacement).
