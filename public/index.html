What makes them ours

Simple to start. Yours to build on.

01

Nothing to install

Every game runs in the browser, on a phone or a computer. Open the link and it is there.

02

Build your own with Claude

Each game is also a connector for Claude. Ask for a level in Free Fall Frank, a place in Crabby Knife Fight, or a look of your own in Bams & Brags.

03

A look and a sound of its own

No two are alike. Every level of Free Fall Frank falls to its own track, the band in Crabby Knife Fight is made as you play, and Bams & Brags has its own rooms, mats and tiles.

04

Free to start

Crabby Knife Fight is free, all of it. Free Fall Frank and Bams & Brags are free to play, with a few things to buy once if you want more. No subscriptions.
